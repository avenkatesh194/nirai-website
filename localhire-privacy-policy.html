LocalHire Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the LocalHire app

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LocalHire (“LocalHire”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). LocalHire is a platform designed to help users discover local job opportunities, connect with employers, and find or offer services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our mobile application and related services. Before publishing, we will ensure this policy reflects the features and data practices actually used by the app.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LocalHire may handle the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal and account information

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Account login and authentication information.
  • Skills, qualifications, work experience, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Information submitted when applying for jobs or contacting employers.

2.2 Location information

With your permission, LocalHire may access your device’s location to show nearby jobs, services, employers, or other relevant opportunities. You may also enter a city, area, or PIN code manually. You can manage location access through your device settings; location-based features may not work if access is disabled.

2.3 Job and service information

We may process information you submit in job postings, applications, service listings, or communications related to opportunities, such as job descriptions, skills, work preferences, and service details.

2.4 Device and technical information

Depending on the services and software development kits integrated into the app, technical information may include device model, operating system, app version, diagnostic information, IP address, network information, app usage, crash reports, and advertising identifiers where applicable.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display local jobs and service opportunities.
  • Allow users to post jobs and submit applications.
  • Connect job seekers, employers, and service providers.
  • Provide location-based recommendations when enabled.
  • Send important account notifications and service updates.
  • Prevent fraud, spam, misuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where applicable.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Storage and Security

Depending on the features used, information may be stored on your device and/or on servers used to operate LocalHire. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes. Information may be shared in these circumstances:

  • With other users: Details included in job postings, service listings, applications, or public profiles may be visible to relevant users.
  • With service providers: We may use hosting, authentication, analytics, notification, and other technical providers to operate the app.
  • For legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, or platform security.
  • At your direction: Information may be shared when you apply for a job, contact an employer, or use a feature that requires disclosure.

Third-party providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Location Permissions

LocalHire may request location permission to help you discover nearby opportunities. Location access is optional unless a particular feature requires it. You can manage or revoke this permission in your device settings. Location information will be handled as described in this policy and in accordance with applicable permissions and law.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

LocalHire may use third-party services, including advertising or analytics providers, depending on the app configuration. These providers may collect technical, usage, or advertising-related information as permitted by their software, your settings, and applicable law.

If Google AdMob or another advertising provider is integrated, its data practices are also governed by the provider’s applicable privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain account functionality, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and enforce our policies.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the details below or by using an account-deletion feature if available. Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security and legal purposes. Information stored locally on your device may remain until you delete it or clear the app’s data.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You can also manage location and other device permissions through your device settings. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children’s Privacy

LocalHire is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law to use the relevant services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information unlawfully, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

LocalHire may contain links to third-party websites, job listings, or services. We do not control the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies before providing information directly to them.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to LocalHire, our data practices, or applicable laws. The updated policy will be published at the same URL with a revised “Last updated” date.

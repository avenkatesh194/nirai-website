My Grocery Lists Privacy Policy

Your simple grocery shopping and expense companion

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to My Grocery Lists ("My Grocery Lists", "we", "us", or "our"). My Grocery Lists is a grocery shopping and expense management application designed to help users create and manage grocery lists, organize items, record prices, track purchases, compare recorded prices, and understand their grocery spending.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information My Grocery Lists handles, how it is stored and used, and how third-party services used by the application may process information.

2. Information You Enter Into My Grocery Lists

My Grocery Lists allows you to enter and manage grocery and shopping information such as:

  • Grocery lists and shopping lists
  • Item names and descriptions
  • Product brands
  • Quantities, units, and package sizes
  • Item prices and purchase prices
  • Grocery categories, such as spices, masalas, oils, nuts, and dals
  • Store or shop information
  • Purchase history
  • Shopping and purchase notes
  • Expense and spending information
  • Budget information, where applicable
  • Other information you choose to enter into the application

3. How Your Grocery Data Is Stored

My Grocery Lists is designed primarily as an offline grocery and shopping management application.

Grocery lists, item information, prices, purchase history, and related expense information that you enter into My Grocery Lists are designed to be stored locally on your device using the application's local storage/database.

My Grocery Lists does not require you to create an account to use its core grocery-list and expense-management functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise provide your personally entered grocery, purchase, or expense records to third parties for their own independent purposes.

Because your grocery and purchase records may be stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security and backups where applicable.

4. Smart Savings and Expense Analysis

My Grocery Lists may provide optional Smart Savings and expense-analysis features using information already recorded in the application.

These features may analyze your grocery and purchase records to calculate information such as:

  • Spending by grocery category
  • Spending by store or shop
  • Spending by individual item
  • Purchase frequency
  • Historical item prices
  • Lowest and highest recorded prices
  • Average recorded prices
  • Monthly, yearly, and period-to-period spending comparisons
  • Potential savings based on your recorded purchase prices
  • Store comparisons based on your recorded purchase prices
  • Shopping-list price comparisons based on recorded purchase data
  • Budget and spending insights, where applicable

Smart Savings results are based on information recorded in the application. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these results do not represent live or guaranteed prices from stores and should not be interpreted as guaranteed savings.

5. Information Collected Automatically

My Grocery Lists may use third-party services that automatically collect certain technical and advertising-related information.

Depending on the services enabled in the application, such information may include:

  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • App version
  • Advertising identifier
  • IP address or approximate location derived from an IP address
  • Advertising and interaction information
  • Diagnostic or technical information

This information may be processed by third-party service providers to provide advertising, prevent fraud, measure advertising performance, diagnose technical issues, and maintain or improve their services.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

My Grocery Lists may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device for purposes including displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and providing personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements.

Advertising identifiers may be used by advertising services to provide and measure advertisements.

The collection and use of information by Google and its advertising services is governed by Google's applicable privacy practices and policies.

7. Advertising Preferences and Consent

Depending on your location and applicable privacy regulations, My Grocery Lists may request your consent regarding personalized advertising and the use of certain information for advertising purposes.

Where required, My Grocery Lists uses Google's consent and privacy mechanisms to obtain and manage applicable advertising consent.

Users may also have device-level or platform-level controls that affect advertising personalization.

8. Data Sharing

My Grocery Lists does not sell your personally entered grocery lists, purchase history, price records, or expense records.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by My Grocery Lists, including advertising and other technical services.

Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

9. Grocery and Expense Information

My Grocery Lists is a grocery shopping, list management, price tracking, and expense analysis tool. It does not provide banking services, hold user funds, process bank transactions, or act as a financial institution.

Expense summaries, budgets, spending analysis, price comparisons, and Smart Savings information are intended to help users understand their own grocery spending and purchase history.

10. Premium Features and In-App Purchases

My Grocery Lists may offer optional premium features or digital functionality through in-app purchases. Premium functionality may include Smart Savings, advanced expense analysis, price comparisons, spending trends, store analysis, and other advanced features.

Purchases made through Google Play are processed by Google Play according to Google's applicable terms and policies. My Grocery Lists does not receive or store your complete payment card details.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by My Grocery Lists. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure, including using appropriate screen locks, passwords, biometric authentication, and operating system security features.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Grocery lists, purchase history, prices, and expense information stored locally by My Grocery Lists generally remain on your device until you delete them through the application, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Because My Grocery Lists' core grocery and expense records are designed to be stored locally, uninstalling the application or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored information.

We recommend exporting or backing up any information you wish to retain before uninstalling the application or clearing its data, where such functionality is available.

13. Children's Privacy

My Grocery Lists is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

14. Third-Party Services

My Grocery Lists may use third-party services to provide functionality such as advertising, payments, diagnostics, security, analytics, or other technical services.

These third parties may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to My Grocery Lists, applicable laws, or third-party services.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

16. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Because My Grocery Lists' core grocery and purchase records are stored locally on your device, many data-management actions can be performed directly within the application or by clearing the application's local data.
